      Answering only the most confident half halves the error — but half of all windows get no answer at all.

      How robust is it to gaps and missing data?

      • 12-hour gaps: excluding windows after a gap longer than 12 hours (30,447 windows flagged, 176,506 kept) gives macro Brier 0.162. The flagged windows are mostly easy, quiet periods, so the remaining set is harder.
      • 30% of events missing: with 30% of events dropped (seed 2207, 23,475,783 of 78,275,425 events), macro Brier is 0.143 — only 0.002 worse than the clean 0.141. The model is nearly insensitive to losing a third of its input.
